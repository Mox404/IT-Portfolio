# Lessons Learned

This project helped me understand how audit evidence supports control decisions. A control should not be marked as effective just because it exists. It needs to be reviewed based on how well it protects the business and whether it is being used correctly.

I also learned why access control and encryption are critical for compliance. If too many people can access sensitive data, or if that data is not encrypted, the organization has a much higher risk of privacy issues and compliance gaps.

Missing backups and disaster recovery plans showed me how cybersecurity connects to business continuity. Security is not only about stopping attacks. It is also about making sure the business can recover when something goes wrong.

This project improved my understanding of GRC because it connected technical controls with business risk. It helped me practice thinking like a security analyst by reviewing evidence, identifying gaps, and recommending practical improvements.
