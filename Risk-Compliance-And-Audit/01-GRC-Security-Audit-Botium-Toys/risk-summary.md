# Risk Summary

## Overall Risk Rating: High

Botium Toys has a high overall risk rating because multiple important controls are missing or weak. The most serious risks involve broad access to internal data, lack of encryption, missing backups, no disaster recovery plan, no intrusion detection system, and weak password management.

## Data Security Risks

Sensitive customer and cardholder data is not encrypted. All employees also have broad access to internally stored data. This increases the chance of unauthorized access, privacy exposure, and compliance issues.

## Compliance Risks

The current environment does not fully align with PCI DSS, GDPR, or SOC Type 1 and SOC Type 2 expectations. The biggest compliance gaps are related to access control, encryption, password management, and data classification.

## Business Continuity Risks

Botium Toys does not currently have disaster recovery plans or critical data backups. This creates a major recovery risk if systems fail, data is lost, or a security incident disrupts business operations.

## Access Control Risks

Least privilege and separation of duties are not implemented. This means employees may have access beyond what their job requires, which increases the risk of misuse, mistakes, or fraud.

## Technical Control Risks

The company has a firewall and antivirus software, but there is no intrusion detection system, no encryption for sensitive data, and no centralized password management system. These gaps reduce visibility and weaken protection for important systems and data.

## Physical Security Posture

Physical security controls are a strength. Locks, CCTV surveillance, and fire detection and prevention systems are in place and functioning.

## Summary

Botium Toys has a useful security foundation, but the missing controls create high business and compliance risk. The first improvements should focus on access control, encryption, backups, disaster recovery, intrusion detection, password management, and data classification.
