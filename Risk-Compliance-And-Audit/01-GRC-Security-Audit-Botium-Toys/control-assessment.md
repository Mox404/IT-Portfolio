# Control Assessment

| Control | Category | Control Type | Status | Evidence / Finding | Risk or Impact | Suggested Improvement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Least privilege | Access management | Administrative | Not implemented | All employees currently have access to internally stored data, including customer and cardholder data. | Broad access increases the chance of unauthorized viewing, misuse, and privacy exposure. | Restrict access based on job responsibilities and business need. |
| Disaster recovery plans | Business continuity | Administrative | Not implemented | No disaster recovery plans are currently in place. | A major outage or incident could interrupt operations and delay recovery. | Create, document, assign ownership for, and test disaster recovery procedures. |
| Password policies | Access management | Administrative | Implemented, needs improvement | A password policy exists, but requirements are minimal. | Weak passwords increase the risk of account compromise. | Strengthen password length and complexity requirements and review them regularly. |
| Separation of duties | Access management | Administrative | Not implemented | Duties are not separated across sensitive business or system functions. | Excessive access can increase the risk of fraud, misuse, or mistakes going unnoticed. | Define role boundaries and separate conflicting duties. |
| Firewall | Network security | Technical | Implemented | A firewall is in place and blocks traffic using defined security rules. | Firewall protection is present, but rules still need regular review. | Maintain firewall rules and review them on a scheduled basis. |
| Intrusion detection system | Network security | Technical | Not implemented | No IDS is currently installed. | Suspicious activity may not be detected quickly. | Deploy an IDS or IDS IPS solution and review alerts regularly. |
| Backups | Business continuity | Technical | Not implemented | Critical data backups are not currently in place. | Data loss could affect operations, recovery, and customer service. | Implement scheduled backups and test restoration procedures. |
| Antivirus software | Endpoint security | Technical | Implemented | Antivirus software is installed and monitored regularly. | Malware protection is present, but it depends on regular updates and monitoring. | Continue monitoring, updating, and reviewing antivirus alerts. |
| Legacy system monitoring and maintenance | System maintenance | Administrative and technical | Implemented, needs improvement | Legacy systems are monitored and maintained, but there is no regular schedule and procedures are unclear. | Unclear maintenance can lead to missed vulnerabilities, outages, or unsupported systems. | Create a maintenance calendar, define procedures, and assign owners. |
| Encryption | Data protection | Technical | Not implemented | Encryption is not currently used for sensitive customer or cardholder data. | Sensitive data exposure could lead to privacy harm and compliance issues. | Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit. |
| Password management system | Access management | Technical | Not implemented | No centralized password management system is currently in place. | Password reuse, weak storage, and poor password sharing practices may occur. | Implement a centralized password manager for approved business use. |
| Locks | Physical security | Physical | Implemented | Offices, storefront, and warehouse have sufficient locks. | Physical access is reasonably controlled. | Continue maintaining locks and review access when staff roles change. |
| CCTV surveillance | Physical security | Physical | Implemented | CCTV surveillance is installed and functioning. | Monitoring supports physical security and incident review. | Continue maintaining CCTV coverage and retention procedures. |
| Fire detection and prevention | Physical safety | Physical | Implemented | Fire detection and prevention systems are functioning. | Fire related risk is reduced. | Continue inspections, testing, and maintenance. |
