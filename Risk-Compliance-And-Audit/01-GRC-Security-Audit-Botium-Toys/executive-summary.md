# Executive Summary

Botium Toys has a high risk security posture because several important controls are missing or need improvement. The most serious gaps are related to access management, encryption, disaster recovery, backups, intrusion detection, password management, and asset classification.

All employees currently have broad access to internally stored data, which may include customer and cardholder information. This creates unnecessary exposure and makes it harder to meet access control and privacy expectations. Sensitive data is also not encrypted, which increases the impact if data is accessed or exposed without authorization.

Business continuity is another major concern. Botium Toys does not currently have documented disaster recovery plans or critical data backups. If an outage, system failure, or security incident occurs, the company may have limited ability to recover operations quickly.

There are also positive controls in place. Botium Toys has a firewall, antivirus software, office locks, CCTV surveillance, fire detection and prevention systems, breach notification procedures, privacy processes, and data integrity controls. These controls provide a useful foundation, but they do not fully address the current risk level.

The recommended next step is to prioritize access control, encryption, backups, disaster recovery planning, intrusion detection, password management, and asset classification. These improvements would reduce security risk, support compliance alignment, and help protect customer trust.
