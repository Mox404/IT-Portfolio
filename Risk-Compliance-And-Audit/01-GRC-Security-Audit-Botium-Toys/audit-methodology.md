# Audit Methodology

This audit followed a simple review process focused on control strength, compliance alignment, and business risk.

## Process

1. Reviewed the audit scope and goals.
2. Reviewed IT managed assets and the business areas affected by those assets.
3. Reviewed current controls and related risk comments.
4. Classified controls as administrative, technical, or physical.
5. Assessed alignment with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC Type 1 and SOC Type 2 best practice expectations.
6. Identified missing or weak controls.
7. Prioritized recommendations based on business risk, data sensitivity, and potential impact.

## Approach

Each control was reviewed based on whether it was implemented, missing, or in place but weak. Findings were then connected to likely risk areas such as unauthorized access, data exposure, compliance gaps, business disruption, and operational weakness.

The final recommendations focus on practical improvements that would give Botium Toys the highest risk reduction first.
